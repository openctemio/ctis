# Patent commitment

This commitment covers the CTIS specification: the text in `docs/spec.md`, the JSON
Schemas in `schemas/` and the conformance vectors in `conformance/`, in every released
version (the **Specification**).

## 1. Commitment

Each Contributor promises not to assert any Necessary Claim it owns or controls, now or
later, against anyone for making, using, selling, offering for sale, importing or
distributing an Implementation, to the extent of that Implementation's conformance to
the Specification.

- **Contributor**: the OpenCTEM Authors, and every person or organisation that
  contributes to the Specification, for the parts they contributed.
- **Necessary Claim**: a patent claim that an Implementation would necessarily infringe
  because it implements a normative part of the Specification, where no technically
  reasonable non-infringing alternative exists. It does not cover enabling technologies
  the Specification only refers to (for example a transport protocol, a hash function or
  a referenced external format), nor the parts of a product that do not implement the
  Specification.
- **Implementation**: any software, hardware or service that produces, receives,
  validates or converts documents conforming to the Specification, in whole or in part.

## 2. Defensive termination

The commitment ends toward a party, and that party's affiliates, that asserts a patent
claim against any Implementation for implementing the Specification. It does not end for
a counterclaim against a party that asserted first.

## 3. Scope and duration

The commitment is irrevocable except under section 2, is free of charge, and binds
successors in title of the Necessary Claims. It applies to every version of the
Specification to which the Contributor contributed, for the parts it contributed.

## 4. Code

Code, schemas and conformance vectors are also licensed under the Apache License 2.0,
whose section 3 grants a patent licence for contributions to that code.

## 5. Status

This statement records the project's commitment in plain terms. The steering group may
replace it by a standard patent agreement for specifications that gives implementers at
least the same protection; such a change follows [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) section 3
and never narrows the protection of a version already released.
